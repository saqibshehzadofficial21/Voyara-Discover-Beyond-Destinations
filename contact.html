A few helpful notes

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers about using Voyara and exploring the collection.

How do I search for destinations?

Use the search field on the home page and enter a category such as beach, temple or country. Singular, plural and related terms are supported.

Are these travel recommendations exhaustive?

No. The collection is a curated sample designed to introduce a selection of destinations, not a complete travel guide.

Can I submit a destination suggestion?

Yes. Use the contact form to prepare an email with your suggestion and any useful context.

Does Voyara book flights or accommodation?

No. Voyara is a destination discovery project and does not provide booking or reservation services.
